## Editor's manifesto (AI-first)

This trailer opens on a **question**, not a logo: *"What if every customer that entered your dealership left intelligence behind?"* We sell with **premium digital craft** — Apple Event pacing, Tesla product reveals, Disney emotional payoff — without a single day on a dealership lot. Human warmth comes from **AI-generated Bolivian families** and **real CPI-OS screens** the audience can verify in a follow-up demo. Music carries the first 85 seconds; VO enters only on the closing card. If an owner checks their phone, we failed.

**Grade arc:** Warm domestic night → cool UI pulse (Acts 3–5) → golden-hour aspiration → black typography.
