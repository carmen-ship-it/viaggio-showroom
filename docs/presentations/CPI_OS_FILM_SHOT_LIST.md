## Director's note — The real WOW move

**Do not open with slides. Open with a movie.**

Before the 20-minute walkthrough, the room watches a **90-second trailer** — no narrator, only music, SFX, and one closing line. Then black. Then the logo. Then the presenter says: *"Ahora veamos cómo funciona."*

The trailer sells harder than 50 slides because owners see **their dealership, their customer, their Monday morning** — not a deck.
