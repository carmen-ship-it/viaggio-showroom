## 1. Editor's Manifesto

This trailer is not a product demo — it is a **lean-forward spell** cast on dealership owners before a single slide appears. We open in the intimate glow of Carla Mendoza's living room in Equipetrol, follow one skeptical family through Viaggio Motors Santa Cruz, and widen the lens until Miguel Ángel Suárez reads Monday's truth on his tablet. Every cut must answer one question and plant the next: *How did they get here? Who knew? What would happen in my showroom?* We withhold all spoken dialogue until the closing typography — only music, ambient room tone, and precisely placed SFX — so the room feels cinema, not PowerPoint. The rhythm accelerates from human warmth (Act 2 grade) through operational pulse (Acts 3–5 cool tech) into golden-hour aspiration and a single question that hands the room to the presenter. If an owner checks their phone during this ninety seconds, we have failed.
