# Product Vision

## Vision Statement

**Viaggio Digital Showroom** transforms the in-dealership experience at Viaggio Motors Bolivia into a premium, self-guided journey — like walking through a Tesla showroom or an Apple Store — where customers discover everything they need to know about GAC vehicles before speaking with a human consultant.

The digital showroom is not a website replacement. It is the **first salesperson**: patient, knowledgeable, always available, and designed to build trust, confidence, excitement, and desire.

## Mission

Equip every visitor to Viaggio Motors (Santa Cruz) with complete product understanding so that human sales consultants spend their time only on:

1. **Financing** — payment plans, trade-in, documentation
2. **Test drive scheduling** — calendar, route, vehicle prep
3. **Closing the sale** — final negotiation and handoff

Everything else — reliability, safety, technology, comfort, warranty, maintenance, value, driving experience — is handled digitally.

## Problem We Solve

### For Customers (Santa Cruz Market)

| Pain Point | Digital Showroom Response |
|------------|---------------------------|
| Distrust of traditional dealership sales pressure | Self-paced exploration with honest, persona-driven guidance |
| Information overload from brochures and spec sheets | Curated, story-driven content organized by decision criteria |
| Uncertainty about Chinese-brand reliability | Carlos (Master Mechanic) addresses engineering and durability directly |
| Family purchase anxiety (safety, space, cost) | Diego models real ownership; Sofía connects features to daily life |
| Preference for WhatsApp over forms | Native WhatsApp CTAs with pre-filled context |
| Limited time at dealership | Efficient path from curiosity to qualified test drive request |

### For Viaggio Motors

| Pain Point | Digital Showroom Response |
|------------|---------------------------|
| Inconsistent product knowledge across staff | Single source of truth for all GAC models |
| Long unqualified conversations | Pre-educated buyers arrive with specific questions |
| Difficulty showcasing technology interactively | Rich media, comparisons, and guided tours |
| Scaling to new models (GS8, EMZOOM, EMKOO) | Content-driven architecture — add vehicle, not rewrite app |

## Target Users

### Primary: In-Dealership Visitors (Santa Cruz)

- Age 28–55, family-oriented SUV buyers
- Middle to upper-middle income
- Research online (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp groups) before visiting
- Value: safety, warranty, resale, fuel economy, brand credibility
- Decision often involves spouse or extended family

### Secondary: Pre-Visit Researchers

- Customers who scan a QR code from social media or WhatsApp before visiting
- Should resume journey seamlessly on in-dealership kiosk or tablet

### Tertiary: Sales Consultants

- Use showroom as presentation aid during live conversations
- Receive lead context (sections viewed, comparisons made, CTA taps)

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Phase 1 Target (Directional) |
|--------|------------|------------------------------|
| **Test drive requests** | Completed scheduling intent via CTA or WhatsApp | Primary KPI — track per session |
| **WhatsApp conversations** | Qualified chat starts with pre-filled vehicle context | Secondary KPI — measure initiation rate |
| **Vehicle sales** | Attributed sales where showroom session preceded close | Lagging KPI — CRM correlation |
| **Session depth** | Avg. sections completed per visit | Engagement quality |
| **Time to CTA** | Minutes from session start to first conversion action | Funnel efficiency |
| **Trust signals consumed** | Views on warranty, safety, Carlos content | Content effectiveness |

## UX Principles

Inspired by Tesla showroom, Apple Store, and luxury automotive — **never** a traditional dealership website.

| Principle | Implementation |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Immersive, not informational** | Full-screen experiences, motion, large imagery — not dense text pages |
| **Guided, not overwhelming** | Progressive disclosure; personas suggest next steps |
| **Trust before desire** | Journey often starts with Carlos (reliability) before Sofía (excitement) |
| **Touch-first** | Large tap targets for kiosk/tablet; works on consultant iPad |
| **Spanish-first** | All UI and voice in natural Bolivian Spanish |
| **WhatsApp-native** | Primary conversion channel, not email forms |
| **Local credibility** | Viaggio Motors branding, Santa Cruz service center, local warranty terms |
| **Multi-vehicle ready** | Vehicle selector subtle but present; GS4 MAX hero at launch |

## Content Themes (Priority Order)

1. **Reliability** — engineering, GAC global presence, local service network
2. **Safety** — ADAS, crash ratings, family protection
3. **Family comfort** — space, climate, infotainment, road trips
4. **Technology** — connectivity, driver assistance, infotainment
5. **Warranty & maintenance** — total cost of ownership, service at Viaggio
6. **Value for money** — feature-to-price vs. segment competitors
7. **Driving experience** — handling, comfort, urban Santa Cruz use cases

## Scope: Phase 1 vs. Future

### Phase 1 (This Documentation)

- Complete planning for GS4 MAX launch experience
- Schemas, content templates, architecture for multi-vehicle extensibility
- Folder and component scaffolding (no application code)

### Phase 2 (Implementation)

- Next.js 15 showroom app
- GS4 MAX full content
- Supabase analytics and lead capture
- In-dealership kiosk deployment

### Phase 3 (Expansion)

- GS8, EMZOOM, EMKOO content packs
- Comparison mode across GAC lineup
- CRM integration (Viaggio sales pipeline)
- Optional pre-visit web entry (QR from social)

## Non-Goals (Phase 1)

- E-commerce or online purchase
- Inventory management system
- Service appointment booking (future consideration)
- English-language primary experience
- Generic multi-brand dealership platform

## Definition of Done (Product)

A visitor leaves the digital showroom able to answer:

- *¿Es confiable este vehículo para mi familia?*
- *¿Qué tecnología y seguridad incluye?*
- *¿Cuánto cuesta mantenerlo y qué garantía tiene?*
- *¿Vale la pena vs. otras opciones en Santa Cruz?*
- *¿Cómo agendo una prueba de manejo?*

…without having asked a salesperson those questions.
