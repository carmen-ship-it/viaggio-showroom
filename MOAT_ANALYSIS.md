## Executive Summary

Viaggio's moat is **not** conversational AI, kiosk UX, or compare tables. Those commoditize in 18 months.

The moat is a **proprietary stack of compounding knowledge assets** — Decision Graph, DNA profiles, outcome-labeled ontology — that turns every customer interaction into institutional memory **no competitor can reconstruct without operating the same closed loop in the same cultural context for the same duration**.

Level 10 (FUTURE_STATE) = platform that learns.  
**Level 11** = **intelligence estate** that compounds faster than competitors can imitate.

# 6. External Critic Perspectives (Q10)

## OpenAI

**Would criticize:**

- Building proprietary reasoning when foundation models will absorb sales conversation  
- Rule-heavy ASI/BIL as interim cruft that won't scale linguistically  
- Underestimating multimodal model cost at 10k+ concurrent graphs  

**Would acknowledge:**

- Grounded Knowledge Library + anti-hallucination as necessary regardless of model  
- Override corpus as fine-tuning gold **if** structured as conversation-outcome pairs  

**Blind spot they won't name:** They need your **outcome-labeled ontology** — you don't need their generic agent.

**Response (intelligence, not feature):** Own the **Decision Graph and DNA layers**; treat LLM as **interchangeable phrasing substrate**.

## Palantir

**Would criticize:**

- No unified ontology governance board — taxonomy drift risk  
- CRM as system of record vs true intelligence graph — integration immaturity  
- Missing formal **lineage and classification** for intelligence objects  
- Single-tenant pilot — no federation architecture for LATAM nodes  

**Would acknowledge:**

- Household decision topology — rare in commercial ontologies  
- MkIE shock registry — good exogenous variable discipline  

**Response:** Elevate **SCDG + CPO** to first-class intelligence products with versioned lineage; CRM is **projection**, not brain.

## McKinsey

**Would acknowledge:**
