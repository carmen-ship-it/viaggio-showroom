## Executive Thesis

Traditional balance sheets record what a company **owns in physical and financial form**. CPI-OS records what a company **knows that compounds** — structured, outcome-linked, auditable intelligence that turns every customer interaction into organizational capital.

The **Intelligence Balance Sheet (IBS)** is the executive instrument that makes this capital visible, measurable, and governable. It answers four questions the P&L and cash flow statement cannot:

1. **What intelligence do we own?** (asset classes and inventory)  
2. **Is it growing or rotting?** (creation rate vs depreciation)  
3. **Does it pay for itself?** (intelligence ROI and assisted margin attribution)  
4. **Can an investor trust the number?** (audit trail, lineage, holdout discipline)  

```
Revenue answers:     "What did we sell?"
Cash flow answers:   "Did we survive?"
IBS answers:         "What did we learn — and is that learning worth more tomorrow?"
```

Level 11 is achieved when the intelligence estate is treated as **balance sheet capital**, not as IT expense or marketing novelty.

# 1. Purpose

### 1.1 What the IBS is

The Intelligence Balance Sheet is a **governance and reporting framework** for CPI-OS intelligence assets. It defines:

- **Asset classes** — the seven proprietary knowledge structures that constitute the intelligence estate  
- **Valuation logic** — how each asset class is scored for depth, quality, and economic linkage  
- **Compounding metrics** — quarter-over-quarter evidence that assets grow faster than competitors can replicate  
- **Attribution rules** — how assisted gross profit flows back to specific intelligence paths  
- **Depreciation triggers** — when intelligence loses economic validity and must be refreshed or written down  
- **Reporting cadences** — what investors, boards, and operators see at each maturity level  

### 1.2 What the IBS is not

| In scope | Out of scope |
|----------|--------------|
| Auditable intelligence inventory | Journal entries, chart of accounts |
| Economic linkage to margin | Tax treatment of intangible assets |
| Lineage and version genealogy | ERP integration specifications |
| Holdout-validated lift claims | Fair value appraisal for M&A purchase price allocation |
| Board and investor narrative | Software feature roadmaps |

### 1.3 Organizational function

The IBS converts CPI-OS from **"we use AI"** into **"we own a compounding intelligence estate."** It gives:

- **CEO / GM** — a single view of whether the company is getting smarter or just busier  
- **Board** — moat evidence independent of unit sales volatility  
- **Investors** — diligence-ready metrics tied to margin, not demo quality  
- **Operators** — clear incentives: label outcomes, preserve lineage, resolve overrides  

### 1.4 Constitutional principle

> **No intelligence asset counts toward the balance sheet without outcome linkage.**

A graph edge without `won | lost | reason` is inventory in draft. A DNA profile without matched close is a hypothesis. An experiment without minimum-N proof is a config change, not capital.

# 2. Why Traditional Financial Statements Miss Intelligence Assets

### 2.1 The accounting blind spot

GAAP and IFRS recognize some intangibles — goodwill, patents, capitalized software — but **operational learning** is expensed as it happens. Marketing spend hits the P&L. Consultant training hits payroll. CRM customization hits IT. The **structured memory** those activities produce never appears as an asset.

CPI-OS intelligence is different from generic "data":

| Generic data | CPI-OS intelligence asset |
|--------------|---------------------------|
| Stored in CRM fields | Typed, versioned, outcome-labeled structure |
| Grows linearly with volume | Compounds through cross-linking (graph × DNA × proof) |
| Copyable via export | Uncopyable without same closed loop + cultural context + duration |
| Depreciates silently (stale) | Depreciation is **measurable** via drift and holdout failure |

### 2.2 What the P&L misstates

| P&L line | What it captures | What it hides |
|----------|------------------|---------------|
| Marketing expense | Ad spend | Marketing DNA — which messages selected which genotypes |
| Sales payroll | Consultant hours | Salesperson DNA — which consultant closes which DNA cluster |
| IT / SaaS | Software subscriptions | Decision Graph — 15k paths of fear → proof → outcome |
| "AI costs" | LLM API fees | Override Corpus — where machines fail in Bolivian trust conversations |

**Result:** A company can show improving revenue while its **intelligence capital decays** — stale ontology, unlabeled losses, proof promotions without holdouts. The P&L looks fine. The moat is hollowing.

### 2.3 What the traditional balance sheet misstates

Physical assets and cash are liquid or depreciable on schedules. Intelligence assets:

- **Appreciate** when outcome labels accumulate and edge weights refine  
- **Depreciate** when market shocks (MkIE) invalidate paths, taxonomy drifts, or competitors change the category  
- **Require governance** — version lineage, experiment genealogy, override resolution — not straight-line amortization  

Treating intelligence as "goodwill from acquisition" misses the point. CPI-OS intelligence is **operational capital created daily** — and destroyed daily if discipline fails.

### 2.4 The investor diligence gap

Private equity and growth investors ask:

- "What's defensible?" → Traditional answer: brand, location, relationships  
- "What compounds?" → Traditional answer: vague "data advantage"  

Without an IBS, diligence devolves into:

- Counting kiosk sessions (engagement fiction)  
- Reviewing LLM vendor contracts (commodity)  
- Auditing CRM hygiene (necessary but insufficient)  

With an IBS, diligence can verify:

- Outcome-labeled graph path inventory  
- Holdout-validated proof lift registry  
- Assisted margin attribution confidence bands  
- Experiment genealogy tied to deployed config  

**The IBS does not replace financial statements. It completes them for intelligence-native companies.**

# 4. Asset Valuation Framework

Valuation in the IBS is **not fair market value for M&A**. It is an **internal capital score** — auditable, conservative, and tied to economic linkage confidence.

### 4.1 Valuation dimensions (per asset class)

Each asset class receives a **Capital Score (0–100)** composed of four dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight | Measures |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| **Depth** | 30% | Inventory volume vs maturity benchmark |
| **Quality** | 35% | Outcome linkage, holdout discipline, consent, CRM truth |
| **Linkage** | 25% | % of margin traceable to this asset class |
| **Freshness** | 10% | Inverse of depreciation flags (see §8) |

```
Asset_Capital_Score = 0.30·Depth + 0.35·Quality + 0.25·Linkage + 0.10·Freshness
```

### 4.2 Depth benchmarks (illustrative — Santa Cruz node, Y3 maturity)

| Asset class | Depth metric | Y1 benchmark | Y3 moat benchmark |
|-------------|--------------|--------------|-------------------|
| Decision Graph | Outcome-labeled edges | 2,000 | 15,000+ |
| Customer DNA | Active profiles | 800 | 12,000+ |
| Salesperson DNA | Calibrated consultants | 5 | 25+ |
| Marketing DNA | Campaigns with outcome join | 10 | 80+ |
| Ontology | Validated nodes | 120 | 400+ |
| Override Corpus | Resolved events | 200 | 2,000+ |
| Experiment Genealogy | Valid experiments | 15 | 120+ |

Depth score = `min(100, 100 × actual / benchmark)` capped at benchmark (no reward for unlabeled volume).

### 4.3 Quality gates (binary disqualifiers)

An asset class **Quality score = 0** if any disqualifier is active:

| Disqualifier | Affected assets |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Outcome label rate < 70% for 2 consecutive quarters | Decision Graph, all DNA classes |
| Zero holdout-validated promotions in trailing 12 months | Experiment Genealogy, Decision Graph |
| Override backlog > 90 days unresolved | Override Corpus |
| Ontology version deployed without changelog | Ontology |
| CRM reconciliation variance > 5% on closes | All margin-linked assets |

### 4.4 Composite Intelligence Estate Value (IEV)

IEV is a **indexed score**, not a dollar figure for external reporting:

```
IEV_index = Σ (w_i × Asset_Capital_Score_i)

Weights (w_i):
  Decision Graph:      0.25
  Customer DNA:        0.18
  Salesperson DNA:     0.12
  Marketing DNA:       0.12
  Ontology:            0.15
  Override Corpus:     0.08
  Experiment Genealogy: 0.10
```

**IEV is published quarter-over-quarter.** Investors see trajectory and composition, not a fabricated dollar intangible.

### 4.5 Economic shadow value (optional executive view)

For internal capital allocation, leadership may compute **Economic Shadow Value (ESV)**:

```
ESV = Σ (Assisted_Margin_Attributed_i × Confidence_Band_i × Persistence_Factor_i)
```

Where:

- `Assisted_Margin_Attributed` — from §7  
- `Confidence_Band` — high (holdout) / medium (quasi-experimental) / low (correlation only)  
- `Persistence_Factor` — expected quarters of validity before depreciation (see §8)  

ESV is **explicitly labeled indicative** — useful for prioritization, not for balance sheet filing.

Compounding is the **proof of moat**. The IBS publishes metrics that demonstrate intelligence grows faster than competitors can imitate.

### 5.1 Primary compounding indicators

| Metric ID | Formula (conceptual) | Target cadence | Moat signal |
|-----------|---------------------|----------------|-------------|
| **CM-01** | Δ outcome-labeled edges / month | Monthly | Graph deepening |
| **CM-02** | Δ DNA profiles with matched outcomes / month | Monthly | Genotype resolution |
| **CM-03** | Holdout-validated proof promotions / quarter | Quarterly | Causal discipline |
| **CM-04** | Assisted margin per labeled path (trailing 90d) | Monthly | Economic linkage |
| **CM-05** | IEV_index QoQ change | Quarterly | Estate-wide health |
| **CM-06** | Match lift (Customer DNA × Salesperson DNA) | Quarterly | Organizational leverage |
| **CM-07** | Marketing DNA waste ratio Δ | Monthly | Acquisition intelligence |
| **CM-08** | Config lineage coverage % | Quarterly | Auditability |

### 5.2 Compounding formula (estate-level)

```
Compounding_Rate(t) =
    α · CM-01_normalized
  + β · CM-03_count
  + γ · CM-04_margin_delta
  + δ · CM-06_lift
  − ε · taxonomy_drift_unresolved
  − ζ · override_backlog_growth
```

Coefficients are calibrated per node; **sign of net rate** matters more than absolute value in early years.

### 5.3 Compounding tiers (from MOAT_ANALYSIS)

| Tier | Assets | Curve |
|------|--------|-------|
| **S — Superlinear** | Decision Graph (holdout paths), Experiment Genealogy, Proof Efficacy | Network + time |
| **A — Linear** | Customer DNA, Salesperson DNA, Marketing DNA, Override Corpus | Volume × quality |
| **B — Step function** | Ontology | Jumps at version milestones |

### 5.4 Compounding stop conditions

Compounding **stops or reverses** when:

1. Outcome labels stop — CRM discipline breaks  
2. Experiments ship without holdouts — intelligence poisons  
3. MkIE shocks unflagged — false attribution inflates then collapses  
4. Key consultant exits before Salesperson DNA institutionalized  
5. Override Corpus ignored — AI errors repeat without calibration  

**CEO rule:** If CM-01 = 0 for 60 days, treat as **capital emergency** — not an analytics gap.

# 6. Intelligence ROI

Intelligence ROI (I-ROI) measures whether the **cost of building and maintaining** the intelligence estate produces **incremental assisted margin** above a defined baseline.

### 6.1 I-ROI definition

```
I-ROI = (Incremental_Assisted_Margin − Intelligence_Operating_Cost) / Intelligence_Operating_Cost
```

**Trailing period:** rolling 12 months (published quarterly).

### 6.2 Incremental Assisted Margin

```
Incremental_Assisted_Margin =
  Assisted_Margin_Actual
  − Assisted_Margin_Counterfactual_Baseline
```

**Counterfactual baseline** must be declared and frozen per fiscal year:

| Baseline type | When used |
|---------------|-----------|
| Pre-CPI-OS same-store | Year 1 pilot |
| Holdout control (10–20% traffic) | Preferred when N sufficient |
| Matched cohort (pre/post with MkIE adjustment) | When holdout infeasible |
| Industry benchmark | Last resort — low confidence |

### 6.3 Intelligence Operating Cost (inclusion rules)

| Include | Exclude |
|---------|---------|
| Outcome labeling labor (allocated) | Generic dealership rent |
| Intelligence review ritual time | Standard sales commission |
| Holdout traffic opportunity cost | OEM co-op not tied to intelligence |
| Data governance and lineage tooling | LLM API (substrate — track separately) |
| Experiment design and analysis | Kiosk hardware depreciation |

### 6.4 I-ROI confidence bands

| Band | Criteria | Reporting |
|------|----------|-----------|
| **High** | Holdout baseline + CRM reconciliation < 3% variance | Publish point estimate |
| **Medium** | Matched cohort + MkIE adjustment | Publish range (±15%) |
| **Low** | Correlation-only or pre/post without control | Publish with disqualifier flag |

### 6.5 I-ROI by asset class (attribution split)

For capital allocation, decompose I-ROI contribution:

| Asset class | Typical attribution method |
|-------------|---------------------------|
| Decision Graph | Margin on holdout-promoted paths vs deprecated paths |
| Customer DNA | Segmented routing lift vs random |
| Salesperson DNA | Match assignment lift |
| Marketing DNA | Waste reduction + genotype-optimized spend reallocation |
| Ontology | Classification error reduction → fewer mis-routes |
| Override Corpus | Error cost avoided post-calibration |
| Experiment Genealogy | Sum of validated experiment lifts |

**Sum of attributions may exceed total** — use Shapley-style normalization for board reporting or report as "gross contribution" with overlap disclosure.

# 7. Assisted Margin Attribution

Assisted Margin is the **economic bridge** between intelligence assets and P&L reality. Without it, the IBS is a library catalog.

### 7.1 Definition

```
Assisted_Margin = Gross_Profit_on_Close
                  WHERE intelligence_assist_flag = true
                  AND attribution_path IS traceable
```

**Intelligence assist** requires at least one:

- Copilot recommendation followed (or consciously overridden with tag)  
- Graph-path routing applied  
- DNA-matched consultant assignment  
- Holdout-promoted proof sequence delivered  

### 7.2 Attribution path (audit chain)

Every assisted close must trace:

```
Campaign (Marketing DNA)
  → Session (Customer DNA assignment)
    → Path (Decision Graph edges traversed)
      → Proofs (Experiment Genealogy version)
        → Consultant (Salesperson DNA match)
          → Outcome (CRM close + gross profit)
            → Overrides (if any — Override Corpus)
```

**Attribution confidence** degrades at each broken link. A close with session but no outcome reason = **low confidence** — counts toward volume, not toward I-ROI numerator.

### 7.3 Assisted Margin Ledger (conceptual)

The Assisted Margin Ledger is the **sub-ledger of the IBS** — not a GAAP ledger.

| Field | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| `closeId` | CRM anchor |
| `assistedMargin` | Gross profit |
| `pathId` | Decision Graph path hash |
| `customerDnaCluster` | Genotype at decision time |
| `salespersonId` | Match record |
| `campaignId` | Marketing DNA source |
| `configVersion` | Experiment Genealogy pointer |
| `confidenceBand` | high / medium / low |
| `mkieShockFlag` | Exogenous event decoupling |

### 7.4 Attribution rules (conservative)

1. **No assist without trace** — untraced closes are organic, not intelligence  
2. **Override reduces confidence, not zero** — human correction is still intelligence-mediated  
3. **MkIE shocks exclude from I-ROI** — Toyota promo month is not proof failure  
4. **Multi-touch split** — default: last intelligence touch 60%, first Marketing DNA touch 25%, graph path 15% (configurable per node)  
5. **Lost deals count** — assisted margin includes **negative learning value** via lost-reason labels  

### 7.5 Reconciliation with finance

Quarterly reconciliation:

| Check | Tolerance |
|-------|-----------|
| Sum assisted margin closes vs CRM gross profit | ≤ 3% variance |
| Assisted close count vs CRM unit count | ≤ 5% variance |
| Unattributed assist flags | < 10% of assisted sessions |

Unresolved variance → **IEV Quality discount** (§4.3).

# 8. Asset Depreciation

Intelligence assets **do not depreciate on a schedule**. They depreciate when **economic validity decays** — measurable, auditable, and reversible through refresh.

### 8.1 Depreciation vs obsolescence

| Type | Trigger | Remediation |
|------|---------|-------------|
| **Drift depreciation** | Ontology node fails holdout; edge weight inverts | Version bump, edge quarantine |
| **Shock depreciation** | MkIE event invalidates path (competitor promo, FX, OEM policy) | Shock flag, path suspension |
| **Stale depreciation** | No new outcome labels on path in 180 days | Re-validation or archive |
| **Poison depreciation** | Experiment promoted without holdout; later rollback | Genealogy mark, IEV penalty |
| **Talent depreciation** | Consultant exit; Salesperson DNA not institutionalized | Capture sprint, match degradation flag |
| **Regulatory depreciation** | Consent model change shrinks DNA cohort | Cohort re-baseline |

### 8.2 Depreciation flags (per asset class)

| Asset class | Primary depreciation signal |
|-------------|----------------------------|
| Decision Graph | Edge weight confidence < threshold; holdout failure |
| Customer DNA | Cluster stale > 12 months; consent shrink |
| Salesperson DNA | Consultant inactive; match lift collapse |
| Marketing DNA | Creative fatigue; genotype mix drift |
| Ontology | `taxonomy_drift_unresolved` count rising |
| Override Corpus | Same override reason recurring post-resolution |
| Experiment Genealogy | Rollback; failed replication |

### 8.3 Depreciation impact on IEV

```
Freshness_score = 100 − (10 × active_depreciation_flags) − (5 × unresolved_drift_nodes)
```

Floor at 0. Multiple flags on same root cause count once if lineage-linked.

### 8.4 Write-down protocol (governance, not accounting)

When depreciation is confirmed:

1. **Quarantine** — path, proof, or cluster removed from active routing  
2. **Document** — Executive Copilot decision packet with margin impact estimate  
3. **Genealogy** — Experiment Genealogy records rollback reason  
4. **Report** — Board queue item if IEV impact > 5 points  

**No silent deletion.** Archived intelligence remains auditable for replication studies.

# 9. Asset Creation Rate

Asset Creation Rate (ACR) measures **how fast new auditable intelligence inventory enters the estate** — the velocity of capital formation.

### 9.1 ACR by asset class

| Asset class | Creation rate metric | Unit |
|-------------|---------------------|------|
| Decision Graph | New outcome-labeled edges / week | edges |
| Customer DNA | New profiles with outcome linkage / month | profiles |
| Salesperson DNA | New consultant × cluster affinities calibrated / quarter | affinities |
| Marketing DNA | New campaigns with full genotype-to-outcome join / month | campaigns |
| Ontology | Nodes validated per ontology review cycle | nodes |
| Override Corpus | New resolved override events / week | events |
| Experiment Genealogy | New valid experiments completed / quarter | experiments |

### 9.2 Gross vs net creation

```
Net_Creation_Rate = Gross_New_Inventory − Depreciated_Archived_Inventory
```

Publish both. Gross flattery hides rot; net reveals true compounding.

### 9.3 Creation efficiency

```
Creation_Efficiency = Net_New_Assisted_Margin / Intelligence_Hours_Invested
```

**Intelligence hours** include: labeling, review rituals, experiment analysis, override resolution, ontology validation.

**Target:** Creation efficiency rises as platform matures — early years are investment-heavy.

### 9.4 Creation rate stoplights

| Signal | Green | Amber | Red |
|--------|-------|-------|-----|
| CM-01 (edges/month) | ≥ benchmark × 0.8 | 0.5–0.8 | < 0.5 |
| Outcome label rate | ≥ 85% | 70–85% | < 70% |
| Net creation (graph) | Positive 3 months | Flat | Negative 2 months |
| Override resolution SLA | < 14 days | 14–30 | > 30 |

Red on any primary signal → **CoIE escalation** within 7 days.

# 12. Moat Measurement

Moat is **not a metaphor**. The IBS defines measurable moat as the **rate and defensibility of intelligence compounding** relative to replicable alternatives.

### 12.1 Moat strength index (MSI)

```
MSI = (IEV_index / 100)
    × (1 + Compounding_Rate_annualized)
    × Defensibility_Factor
    × min(1, years_operational / 3)
```

**Defensibility_Factor (0.5–1.0):**

| Factor | Score |
|--------|-------|
| Holdout discipline active | +0.15 |
| Override Corpus > 1,000 resolved | +0.10 |
| Ontology v3+ floor-validated | +0.10 |
| Second node or vertical live | +0.15 |
| CRM reconciliation clean | +0.10 |
| Base | 0.50 |

Cap at 1.0.

### 12.2 Moat vs competitor clock

| MSI | Interpretation |
|-----|----------------|
| < 0.3 | Pre-moat — feature race |
| 0.3–0.6 | Emerging — 18–36 month copy clock |
| 0.6–0.8 | Defensible — 3+ year honest replication |
| > 0.8 | Category — licensable methodology |

### 12.3 Moat evidence checklist (Y3 "proven")

- [ ] ≥ 15,000 outcome-labeled graph paths  
- [ ] ≥ 3 holdout-validated proof promotions in trailing 12 months  
- [ ] Customer DNA match lift statistically significant on ≥ 2 clusters  
- [ ] Marketing DNA closes acquisition-to-margin loop  
- [ ] Experiment Genealogy links ≥ 80% of production config  
- [ ] Competitor parallel entry estimated > 36 months for equivalent estate  

### 12.4 Moat erosion early warnings

| Signal | Implication |
|--------|-------------|
| CM-01 flat while competitor enters market | Race to commoditize |
| Rising override rate on same clusters | AI substrate catching up; structure not compounding |
| Salesperson DNA concentration in 2 consultants | Talent risk masquerading as moat |
| Ontology drift unresolved > 90 days | Classification moat leaking |
