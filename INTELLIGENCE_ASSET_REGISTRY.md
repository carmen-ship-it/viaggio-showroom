## Executive Summary

CPI-OS compounds through **thirteen canonical knowledge assets (KA-01 through KA-13)**. Each asset is outcome-linked, auditable, and governed. Together they form the **intelligence estate** that Level 11 measures and the Intelligence Balance Sheet (IBS) capitalizes.

This registry is the **single diligence inventory** for boards, investors, and federation partners. It resolves prior inconsistencies across moat analysis, IBS scoring, federation transfer rules, DNA engines, and the Executive Recommendation Engine (ERE).

```
Interaction → labeled paths (KA-01) → typed vocabulary (KA-02)
    → household + genotype (KA-03, KA-11) → proof lift (KA-06)
    → consultant match (KA-04) → acquisition selection (KA-05)
    → exogenous discipline (KA-09) → margin trace (KA-07)
    → human calibration (KA-08) → config lineage (KA-10)
    → executive action (KA-13) → federation layers (KA-12)
```

**Constitutional principle (unchanged):** No asset counts toward capital without outcome linkage.

## KA-01 — Santa Cruz Decision Graph (SCDG)

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **Description** | Typed multigraph of fears → proofs → actions → outcomes in Bolivian high-trust considered-purchase context. Atomic institutional memory: not a funnel, not a journey map. |
| **Owner** | **CoIE Steward** (integrity, holdouts) + **GM Commercial** (outcome label rate) |
| **Creation mechanism** | Every labeled interaction + CRM close creates or strengthens outcome-labeled edges. PathInstances from kiosk, WhatsApp, floor, and copilot sessions. |
| **Learning mechanism** | Weekly graph diff; edge weight refinement from outcome correlation; holdout-disciplined promotion (feeds KA-06); MkIE shock adjustment (`mkie_shock_adj`); backpropagation to KA-03/04/05. |
| **Depreciation mechanism** | Edge weight confidence below threshold; holdout failure; 180-day stale path without new labels; MkIE shock invalidation; evidence_count < N overfitting guard. |
| **Transferability** | **Layer 0:** full weights local only. **L1+:** topology + edge *types* transfer; weights re-learned to minimum-N (Federation FP-02). Never export raw Santa Cruz weights to L2 without holdout. |
| **Moat contribution** | Primary compounding surface. α term in moat formula: `\|edges with outcome labels\|`. 15k+ paths at Y3 = 3-year replication barrier. |
| **IBS capitalization treatment** | **Core asset — 25% IEV weight.** Lines: `graph_edges_labeled`, `graph_edges_holdout_validated`, `graph_path_margin_coverage`. Tier S. |
| **Investor explanation** | *"We own a living map of what moves Bolivian buyers from fear to close — every edge outcome-labeled and holdout-tested. Competitors can copy screens; they cannot copy three years of honest lost-reason data in the same cultural context."* |
